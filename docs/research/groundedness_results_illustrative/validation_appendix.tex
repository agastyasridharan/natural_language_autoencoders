\section{Groundedness Validation and Reporting Details}
\label{app:grounding-validation}

\begin{quote}
\small\textbf{Draft protocol supplement.} The primary-study details below
follow the saved protocol where specified. The independent validation studies
are proposals. Their design choices must be frozen before inspecting their
outcomes; none of their numerical results has been measured in this draft.
\end{quote}

\subsection{Reconstruction geometry and comparison scores}

For the released checkpoint, define
\begin{equation}
 \widehat{a}(z)=\frac{\operatorname{AR}(z)}{\|\operatorname{AR}(z)\|_2},
 \qquad
 E_{\mathrm{dir}}(z;h)=\|\bar h-\widehat{a}(z)\|_2^2,
\end{equation}
where $\bar h=h/\|h\|_2$. This is the reconstruction-metric amendment recorded
in the experiment log. The same geometry must be used for claim-deletion
scores, reconstruction-matching filters, and any FVE calculation that uses
these predictions. Directional FVE uses the variance of unit-normalized targets
as its denominator. Raw-prediction MSE and directional MSE must not be mixed
in a table or pooled analysis.

The absolute claim-deletion score is
\begin{equation}
B_i^{\mathrm{del}}=E_{\mathrm{dir}}(z_{-i};h)-E_{\mathrm{dir}}(z;h).
\end{equation}
Because longer claims may contain more information, report claim-length
strata and a prespecified per-token variant as sensitivity analyses. Neither
variant is chosen after inspecting which gives the less favorable baseline.
The baseline and the proposed method use identical claims, exact deletion
spans, target activations, and AR outputs.

The score $G_i$ measures specificity relative to a decoy family. It is not a
probability that a claim is true. In particular, exceeding an empirical 95th
percentile of 20 selected decoys is not automatically a level-0.05 statistical
test. The decoys are dependent, selected, and not exchangeable with an
AV-generated claim. Error rates must be estimated on independent controls.

\subsection{Primary classification and unresolved cases}

The decision rule is sequential. A valid claim that passes specificity is
grounded if it also passes attribution and entangled otherwise. A claim that
fails specificity proceeds to the thematic-support gate, irrespective of
the attribution gate. It is seeded if thematic support exceeds its threshold;
otherwise it is confabulated only if activation-specific support is also low.
The weak-theme/high-activation-support branch remains unresolved. This
completes the branch omitted by the phrase ``fails both tests'' in the
initial manuscript.

The category ``confabulated'' consequently means that the stated tests fail
to detect support, not that an absence theorem has been established. A small
$R_i$ alone never supplies this label. Near-zero reconstruction differences,
nonfinite outputs, ambiguous token-span assignments, generation failures,
and inadequate decoy families require separate accounting. Thresholds for
numerical degeneracy must be specified before inspecting the evaluation set.

Report a flow table containing attempted documents or trajectories, available
activations, usable explanations, inventoried claims, sampled claims, valid
directions, complete decoy families, and each final category. Report both raw
counts and weighted estimates. If a failure occurs before claims can be
inventoried, it cannot be assigned a fictitious number of claim-level unresolved
labels: report it separately at the activation level and quantify the resulting
selection limitation. Within unresolved claims, distinguish numerical failure,
failed matching, and the residual decision branch.

The saved primary protocol uses 64 candidate decoys, exactly 20 selected
controls, and the fixed relative overlap ladder
$0.10,0.20,0.35,0.50,0.75,1.00$. Report which rung was required, retained
projection ratios, baseline-score differences, and reconstruction differences.
An effect that appears only after very permissive matching warrants a weaker
interpretation. Sensitivity analyses may restrict to tighter matches but must
show the accompanying coverage change and cannot replace the primary estimate.

\subsection{Independent controlled-pair validation}

The proposed benchmark uses counterbalanced minimal pairs. Each pair changes
one attribute while preserving its broad context and keeping the activation
read position comparable. Use held-out templates and instances; split all
variants of a template together. Generate explanations and define eligible
attribute claims before computing any verification score. If two opposing
claims are selected, score each under both activations, producing four cases
per pair. The numerical example in the results assumes 300 pairs, 1,200
attempted cases, and 1,056 jointly scorable cases.

Select naturally occurring claim-bearing explanations where possible. Scoring
the same explanation under the opposite activation is a deliberate transport
test, not a fresh sample from that activation. Report whether the originating
explanation was source-consistent and whether the selected attribute was
actually verbalized; do not silently exclude a wrong original explanation.
If fixed templates or inserted candidate claims are used instead, label that
as a separate benchmark because it changes the distribution of text supplied
to the AR. Report spontaneous-claim recall separately from verification
accuracy so that a verifier is not credited for concepts the AV never reports.

Three safeguards strengthen the labels. First, verify the attribute value from
the constructed context without consulting the NLA. Second, fit a simple
contrastive linear readout on disjoint templates and evaluate whether the
attribute is decodable at the chosen site. Third, test the predefined downstream
attribute contrast in the target model. Report these measurements for all
pairs rather than selecting pairs on the proposed score. Decodability does
not establish natural causal use; behavioral sensitivity does not prove that
every verbalized detail is represented.

Freeze separate benchmark operating thresholds on a disjoint calibration set.
These are for comparing continuous verifiers at a common target false-positive
rate; they do not recalibrate the primary five-category classifier. Report
AUROC, AUPRC, TPR, FPR, coverage, and the paired difference from claim deletion.
The primary AUROC contrast is $G_i$ versus $B_i^{\mathrm{del}}$ on common
eligible cases. Bootstrap whole template families, retaining both context
conditions and all claims from a pair in every resample. Report discrimination
separately by attribute type and origin condition.

\subsection{SAE compatibility, coverage, and annotation}

Record the exact SAE checkpoint, target-model revision, layer, hook site,
input normalization, dictionary size, sparsity, and held-out reconstruction
quality. No compatible SAE is assumed to exist merely because an SAE with
the same model-family name is available. If a compatible dictionary cannot be
obtained or trained, omit the quantitative SAE results and state that this
validation remains pending.

Feature-to-claim mappings are established using independent feature examples,
including negative examples, rather than only a feature's short label. Fix the
search budget and retrieval procedure before evaluation. A feature that
describes ``politics'' does not establish coverage for ``Senate filibuster.''
If multiple features jointly express a proposition, specify their combination
before scoring and use the same rule across conditions. Freeze activity
thresholds from independent SAE calibration activations; do not tune them to
maximize agreement with $G_i$.

For the controlled comparison, map propositions to features once and keep
that mapping identical across the two activation conditions. A candidate
continuous SAE score is a fixed signed aggregation of independently
standardized feature activations supporting the claimed value versus its
alternative. The aggregation and standardization corpus must be recorded.
If no validated mapping exists, the SAE abstains: assigning a zero score would
confound lack of coverage with negative evidence.

For natural claims, blinded annotators separately judge whether an exact
mapping exists, whether its features are active, and whether active features
support only a broader theme. Category-balanced sampling supports comparisons
between categories but requires sampling weights for population estimates.
Record pre-adjudication agreement and the adjudication procedure. Because
multiple claims may share a document, proposition, or SAE feature, report
document-clustered intervals and a sensitivity analysis clustering by concept
or feature. SAE corroboration is convergent evidence, not an independent
ground-truth label for every disagreement.

\subsection{Interventions in the target model}

To match the original normalized-space projection at a target residual-stream
site with raw activation $h^{\mathrm{raw}}$, implement
\begin{equation}
 h^{\mathrm{raw,abl}}=h^{\mathrm{raw}}
 -(h^{\mathrm{raw}\top}\widehat\Delta_i)\widehat\Delta_i.
\end{equation}
This is equivalent to multiplying the projected unit vector by the original
raw norm. Do not apply the AV injection scale to the target model, and do not
renormalize the ablated target activation. Apply the edit only at the specified
site; all other activations arise from the ensuing target-model computation.

Use full forward passes for interventions that must affect downstream states;
do not accidentally reuse a cache computed from the unmodified activation.
Counterbalance answer labels and use a prespecified sequence log-likelihood
contrast when outcomes require multiple tokens. The behavioral outcome must
not be an AV judgment of its own explanation.

For each own-direction edit, retain the same decoy controls and add random
controls with matched perturbation norm. Measure prespecified unrelated
outcomes and continuation degradation. Sweep a small fixed grid of intervention
strengths as a secondary analysis, including zero; choose no strength on
held-out behavioral success. Report the original projection dose separately
from any subsequently optimized steering result.

Category-conditioned sampling estimates effects within the sampled task
domain. It does not establish population causal prevalence. The synthetic
table assumes 40 eligible claims in each category; if a category does not
contain that many eligible tasks, report its actual size rather than relaxing
eligibility after seeing the scores.

\subsection{Sensitivity, selection effects, and uncertainty}

The following checks address identifiable failure modes rather than seeking
the most favorable variant. Retain the frozen primary analysis in every case.

\paragraph{Teacher-forced context.}
The prefix $z_{<t}$ can already reveal a later claim. Compare the original
scoring protocol with an independently specified, minimal-prefix version on
a fixed subset, and stratify by whether earlier text entails the claim. A
lower activation effect after prefix leakage need not indicate hallucination.
The minimal-prefix analysis changes the estimand and should be reported as
a sensitivity analysis, not silently substituted for the primary score.

\paragraph{Reference activation and activation specificity.}
Compare the calibration mean with a fixed panel of held-out reference
activations. A mean activation need not resemble any naturally occurring
activation. Recompute reference-dependent quantities consistently in each
variant. A shuffled-activation control should keep the original explanation
fixed, then transport it to another activation; regenerating an explanation
from the new activation is not the same null experiment.

\paragraph{Claim decomposition and ownership.}
Repeat the analysis on a blinded alternative segmentation for a fixed subset.
The maximum competing-claim effect grows sensitive to the number and overlap
of claims in the explanation. Report grounded-plus-entangled rates alongside
ownership rates, stratify by claim count, and state whether an alternative
ownership statistic changes the grounded/entangled split. Do not replace the
frozen maximum rule with a percentile rule after inspecting results.

\paragraph{Control selection.}
Report sensitivity to removing the activation-support and reconstruction
filters separately. They condition on quantities related to the proposed
outcome and baseline, respectively. Also report results by matching-rung
strictness, claim length, baseline propensity, and perturbation magnitude.
The finite decoy pool makes the upper quantile noisy: repeated selection
within a frozen pool assesses selection stability, while independently
generated pools assess a broader source of uncertainty. These are distinct.

\paragraph{Inference and computational cost.}
Use paired cluster-bootstrap differences rather than judging differences by
overlap of marginal intervals. Fix the primary contrasts before outcomes and
label the remaining subgroup comparisons exploratory; specify multiplicity
handling if hypothesis-test claims are made. Provide actual GPU time, AV/AR
forward-pass counts, text-model calls, and cache reuse. Claim deletion reuses
the AR contrasts already computed by the proposed method; its incremental
evaluation cost is therefore small, whereas matched-decoy scoring is not.
No runtime or significance claim is supplied by the synthetic numbers.
