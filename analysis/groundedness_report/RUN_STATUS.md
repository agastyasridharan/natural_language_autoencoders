## Approved reconstruction-metric amendment

The supplied appendix defines

`E(z; h) = ||h - AR(z)||^2`,

where `h` is unit normalized. The released AR was trained after normalizing
both its prediction and the target, and `NLACritic.reconstruct()` returns the
raw prediction. Consequently, the raw AR output norm is unidentified by the
training objective. In a FineWeb diagnostic, raw AR norms were 202--212 while
`||h|| = 1`; the report's raw error was 40,600--44,600, whereas directional
error after normalizing both vectors was 0.26--0.31.

Before any calibration threshold or category percentage was generated, the user
approved the correction

`E_dir(z; h) = ||unit(h) - unit(AR(z))||^2 = 2(1 - cosine(h, AR(z)))`.

This matches the released checkpoint's training geometry and its documented
inference procedure. It is a substantive departure from the literal appendix
equation and is therefore recorded as the immutable pre-outcome amendment
`audits/v7/reconstruction_metric_amendment.json`, SHA-256
`3ec1a6fb1f6668f00d83caf48fd033d2d8e155b1021a25806a3587403c839057`.
The original preregistration remains byte-identical. New AR-error caches,
measurements, calibrations, verdicts, and summaries must carry the amendment
hash; pre-amendment reconstruction errors cannot be reused. Upstream trajectory,
activation, AV-verbalization, segmentation, and decoy-text artifacts are
unaffected.

The amended implementation passed all 88 tests on Athena, including the live
embedding-hook test. A non-outcome H100 smoke test against the pinned AR revision
gave raw `||AR(z)|| = 209.4663` and amended directional error `0.26466275` for
FineWeb audit record 16. The exact input hash and result are recorded in
`audits/v7/reconstruction_metric_gpu_smoke.json`. No threshold or category
verdict was produced.
